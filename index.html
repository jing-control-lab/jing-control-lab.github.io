About the Group

Jing Control Lab is a space for exploring the links between models, decisions, and feedback. We are interested in both the mathematical foundations of control and the practical questions raised by interconnected systems.

This website will bring together our research directions, technical notes, and future group updates.

Research Overview

Our guiding questions are how to learn useful models from data, how agents can coordinate with limited information, and how feedback can support reliable decisions in changing environments.

Across these questions, we consider uncertainty, shared constraints, and the relationship between local actions and system-wide behavior.
